### 1. Pre-Boarding Logistics

* **Hardware & accounts** – laptop, 2FA setup, VPN setup, pager/on-call phone.
* **HR & mandatory training** – HIPAA/security awareness, hospital policies.
* **Software licenses** – IDE, paid SaaS tooling, AWS/Azure credits if applicable.[^1]
